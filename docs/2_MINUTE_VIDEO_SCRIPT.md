## 🎥 Recording & Presentation Tips to Win

1. **High Contrast Display**: Keep the browser in dark mode with full screen recording (`1920x1080` or `1440p`).
2. **Audio Quality**: Use a crisp microphone (even Apple AirPods or a USB mic) with zero background noise.
3. **Smooth Mouse Movements**: Move your cursor deliberately when clicking the defect so judges can follow the point-prompt interaction clearly.
4. **Drag the Slider Confidently**: When showing the Before/After slider, slide it smoothly from 60% to 10% to 90%—hackathon judges love interactive visual comparisons.
